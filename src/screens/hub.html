How this app is put together

Same material as ever, arranged so the navigation actually means something.

The four screens

Task first, not chapter first

Job Hub is the run-up to a job and the job itself. Support is for the moment something is wrong or you need a number. The pathway is the long game. The library is everything, in order, underneath.

Everything is searchable

One index, all 21 parts

Search reaches every word of the handbook and every one of these screens. Open it from Library in the bottom bar, or press / on a keyboard.

It is yours

Notes, ticks and themes stay on this device

Section notes, checklists, the job workspace and your chosen colours are saved only in this browser and never sent anywhere. Export a backup now and then from Appearance.

What's in this pack

The full contents, in one sentence

Running a job and the people around you, core audio fundamentals, RF, PA and networked systems, working across different departments and event types, d&b systems and speakON cabling, fault-finding and working safely, recording and mixing craft, and career, learning and field reference — checklists, glossary and calculators, all in one place.
